# Pages excluded from indexing
#
# Handled by <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> on the pages themselves
# (Impressum, Datenschutz, AGB and their EN counterparts, plus 404.html) —
# NOT by Disallow. Two reasons the previous Disallow block was removed:
#
#   1. It sat directly under "User-agent: CCBot", so by the grouping rules of
#      robots.txt it applied to Common Crawl alone. Googlebot matched the
#      "User-agent: *" group at the top and was never affected.
#   2. Disallow and noindex work against each other. A crawler that may not
#      fetch a page cannot read its noindex, so the URL can still surface in
#      results — just without a snippet. Letting crawlers fetch the page and
#      obey its noindex is what actually keeps it out of the index.
